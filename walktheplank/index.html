🏴‍☠️ How It Works

  • Pick yer party: Republican, Democrat, or Independent. The captain marches ye to the end of the plank — blindfolded (it slips, don't tell him).
  • Party planks float past one at a time: Republican 🐘, Democrat 🐴, Independent 🧭… and the occasional Communist 🚩 gag plank (the captain finds it hilarious).
  • Is the plank yer party's? Hit ⚓ GRAB IT. Not yers? Hit ⛵ LET PASS. Read the tag, read the plank, decide fast.
  • Grab 5 of yer own planks → build a raft and sail to freedom! 🛟
  • Blunder (grab a wrong-party plank — including Commie planks — or let yer OWN plank float by) → the captain's sharks circle. 🦈 3 blunders and ye SINK.
  • The plank timer gets shorter every round. No pressure. Arrr.
